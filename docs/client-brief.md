# Client brief – Gemstone Selections

Source: client brief pasted into the working session (second brief, after M2). Saved verbatim for reference.

---

Enhance the existing Gemstone Selections website by designing two complete, connected experiences: Buyer Experience and Seller Experience.

## 1. BUYER EXPERIENCE

Create a premium gemstone discovery experience.

- When buyers open the website, first show top-rated and verified sellers in a horizontal scrolling section, including business name, ratings, reviews, location and specialisation.
- Below that, show featured gemstones in another horizontal scrolling section.
- Buyers can browse gemstone categories, search and filter products.
- Clicking a seller opens their complete business profile and portfolio, displaying all their available gemstones.
- Clicking a gemstone opens a dedicated product detail page with images, specifications, pricing, certificates, valuation reports and seller information.
- From the product page, buyers can view the seller's portfolio, save the gemstone to their wishlist, request valuation for that specific gemstone and chat or enquire with the seller.
- Buyers can also post their gemstone requirements so relevant sellers can respond.
- Include a personal buyer dashboard for wishlists, enquiries, conversations, valuations and saved reports.

## 2. SELLER EXPERIENCE

Create a complete seller onboarding and business management experience.

- Add a "Become a Seller" option.
- Sellers submit an application with their name, business name, email, contact details, location, gemstone categories, business information and required documents.
- Admin reviews the application and approves or rejects it.
- After approval, sellers receive secure account activation and access to their own seller dashboard.
- Sellers can create and manage their business profile and portfolio.
- Allow sellers to upload gemstone products with images, videos, descriptions, specifications, pricing, categories and availability.
- Allow sellers to upload certificates, SSL-related options, gemstone valuation reports and laboratory documents.
- Admin can review listings and documents and manage their verification status.
- Sellers can manage buyer enquiries, quotations and conversations through the platform.
- Include seller analytics showing profile visits, product views, enquiries and performance.

## 3. MEMBERSHIP & GROWTH PROGRAMME

Initially, seller registration and basic membership will be FREE.

Introduce future membership levels:

- Free Membership
- Premium Membership
- Elite Membership

Sellers can unlock additional benefits, visibility and features based on eligible referrals, subscriber growth and platform engagement. Show membership progress and upcoming benefits inside the seller dashboard. Keep paid upgrades and rewards as upcoming demo features.

## 4. ADMIN EXPERIENCE

Create an admin dashboard to manage seller applications, account approvals, products, gemstone categories, certificates, reports, document verification, memberships, referrals, buyer enquiries and platform activity.

## 5. COMPLETE CONNECTED USER JOURNEY

Buyer Journey: Homepage → Discover Sellers → Explore Portfolios → Browse Gemstones → Product Details → Wishlist / Valuation / Chat / Enquiry.

Seller Journey: Become a Seller → Submit Application → Admin Approval → Account Activation → Seller Dashboard → Create Portfolio → Upload Gemstones & Documents → Admin Review → Publish Products → Receive Buyer Enquiries.

A seller should also be able to use buyer features to explore and enquire with other sellers.

## DESIGN & FUNCTIONALITY

Maintain the existing premium, minimal and professional Gemstone Selections design. Use elegant layouts, high-quality gemstone imagery, horizontal discovery carousels, clean dashboards and responsive pages.

Make every page connected through working demo navigation and interactions. Use realistic sample data, but clearly label demo ratings, reviews and verification statuses. Do not expose sellers' private contact information publicly. Keep buyer-seller communication within the platform.

Build upon the existing website and preserve its current design direction instead of creating a completely new website.
